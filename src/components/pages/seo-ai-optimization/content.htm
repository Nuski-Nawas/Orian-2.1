What is AEO? Answer Engine Optimization Explained

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking in Google's blue-link results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) goes further — it optimises your content to be selected as the answer by AI search engines when users ask questions in natural language.

As of 2026, over 40% of search queries are being answered directly by AI without users clicking traditional search results.
