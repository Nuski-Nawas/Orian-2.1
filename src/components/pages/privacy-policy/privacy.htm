Data protection guidelines

Orianwave is a digital agency providing services including website development, SEO, branding, digital marketing, and POS system solutions. We are committed to protecting your personal and business data while ensuring transparency in how information is collected and used.

Orianwave privacy policy

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our website, services, or communicate with us for business inquiries and project collaborations.

Personal information we collect

When you visit our website or use our services, we may collect certain information such as your name, contact details, project requirements, and technical data like browser type and device information. This helps us deliver better services and improve user experience.

  • Cookies – Used to enhance user experience, remember preferences, and analyze website performance.
  • Log Files – Collect basic data such as IP address, browser type, and activity for security and analytics.
  • Tracking Technologies – Used to understand how visitors interact with our website and improve performance.
  • Analytics Tools – Tools like Google Analytics help us monitor traffic and optimize our services.

How we use your personal information

We use the collected information to:

  • Communicate with you regarding projects and inquiries
  • Deliver and manage our services
  • Process payments and generate invoices
  • Improve customer experience

We also use this data to:

  • Analyze performance and optimize website functionality
  • Monitor security and prevent fraud or misuse
  • Provide relevant updates, offers, or service-related information

Sharing your personal information

We only share your information with trusted third-party services when necessary to operate our business efficiently:

  • Analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics)
  • Payment providers
  • Hosting providers

Do not track
